# Ethical risk register
| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Re-identification or small cells | Exclude direct identifiers and text, generalize geography, and pool cells below 100 orders into a residual group. |
| Biased or incomplete labels | Report review coverage and regional representation. Protected-group analysis is not supported by this dataset. |
| Misuse as seller judgment | Investigation-only outputs; human review. |
| Regulatory mismatch | Review scope and jurisdiction before use. |
