# KPI definitions
- On-time rate: delivered orders on/before estimate divided by eligible delivered orders.
- Late rate: delivered orders after estimate divided by eligible delivered orders.
- Mean days vs estimate: actual delivery date minus estimated delivery date, averaged by segment. Negative values mean the order arrived early.
- Low-review order share: orders with an average numeric review score ≤2 divided by delivered orders with a numeric review score.
- Review coverage: delivered orders with a numeric review score divided by eligible delivered orders.
