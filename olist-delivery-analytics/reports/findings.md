# Initial descriptive findings

For the full eligible population (96,470 delivered orders), the weighted late-delivery rate was 8.11%. Mean actual delivery occurred 11.18 days before the estimate. The share of orders with an average numeric review score of 2 or lower was 12.77%, and numeric review coverage was 99.33%.

These values describe the historical Olist snapshot. They do not explain why delays occurred and should not be used to assign fault. The detailed aggregate cells cover 39,223 orders; the remaining 57,247 orders are in a pooled residual group for privacy. Use pooled rows for totals only. Further causal analysis would require additional operational data, such as carrier handoff timestamps and fulfillment events.
